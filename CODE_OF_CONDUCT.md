# Code of conduct

## Our promise

We, the members, contributors and maintainers, promise to make participation in our community free
of harassment for all persons. This promise does not change with age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression. It also does not change
with level of experience, education, social and economic status, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We promise to act in ways that help an open, friendly, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Correct behavior

- Show empathy and kindness to other persons.
- Respect opinions, points of view and experiences that are different from yours.
- Give feedback that helps, and accept feedback that helps you.
- Accept responsibility for your errors. Apologize to the persons that your errors affect, and
  learn from them.
- Think about what is best for the full community, not only for you.

## Behavior that is not acceptable

- Sexual language or images, and sexual attention or advances of any type.
- Comments that try to start fights, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publication of private information of a person without their permission, for example a physical
  address or an email address.
- Other conduct that is not correct in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities of the maintainers

The maintainers make the standards of behavior clear and enforce them. They take fair action when
behavior is not acceptable.

The maintainers can remove, edit or refuse comments, commits, code, wiki edits and issues that do not
agree with this code of conduct. They tell the reason when it is necessary.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all community spaces. It also applies when a person represents the
community in public spaces. For example: an official email address, an official social media
account or an event as a representative of the project.

## Report a problem

Report behavior that is not acceptable to the maintainers. Send a private message to the owner of
the repository on GitHub, or use the email address on the GitHub profile of the owner. The
maintainers examine each report quickly and fairly. They keep private the identity of the person who
reports.

## Enforcement

The maintainers use these steps to decide the consequences of a violation:

| Step | Behavior | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Correction | Language that is not correct, or other behavior that is not professional. | A private, written warning that tells the problem. A public apology can be necessary. |
| 2. Warning | One violation, or a series of actions. | A warning with consequences. No contact with the persons in the problem for a specified time. |
| 3. Temporary ban | A serious violation, or behavior that continues. | A ban from all contact with the community for a specified time. |
| 4. Permanent ban | Behavior that continues and violates community standards, harassment of a person, or aggression against groups of persons. | A permanent ban from all public contact with the community. |
